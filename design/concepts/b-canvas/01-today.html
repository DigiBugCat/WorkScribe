Space is the filing systemNo folders, tabs or a home dashboard. Where you put things is how you organise them; WorkScribe only proposes (ghost cards) and auto-clusters loose cards by topic if you ask.
Every kind of thing is a cardA PDF page, a second of audio, a video frame, a turn someone said, a photo, a web clip from Obsidian's Web Clipper, a person. They all connect the same way.
Prep is a pull, not a pageBefore a call the assistant pulls cards next to “Up next” with weak dotted links. Keep turns them solid; ignoring them lets them fade after the call.
Stored in your vaultYour notes are Markdown in your Obsidian vault with [[links]]; the canvas itself could be a JSON Canvas (.canvas) file there too, so Obsidian can open it.
