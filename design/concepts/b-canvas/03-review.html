After the call, the region settles

When you press Stop, the region rearranges itself from time order into topics. It shows a brief, your enhanced notes as decisions, who owes what (hanging off each person's card), what was said but not noted, and the answers you got live. Because the meeting is part of the canvas, the review can also show what it changed elsewhere: a promise in the kickoff that's now out of date, a gap in the contract. Arrange can switch to Time, or to Document for a plain reading view.
