A live call is a region that grows

Pressing Start under “Up next” opens a region on the canvas and it grows downward with the call. A time spine runs down its left edge. Each jot from the pinned jot bar lands as a blush card at the spine's current edge. Its enhanced version paints in beside it, linked to the transcript lines it drew from: solid links for “time + meaning”, dotted ones for “meaning only”. Detected questions are answered in the assist lane on the right, linked to the cards they cite wherever those already sit on your canvas.
