the call stays in front

  1. One line, always on top. ⌥⌘J focuses it from any app; ⏎ files the jot into the live block and returns focus to the call.
  2. One echo line. The last enhancement shows as a single dimmed row, so you know it landed without reading it now.
  3. One answer row. A live answer is a row, not a card. ⌥⌘O opens it in the main window’s sheet.
  4. Drop a link to clip. A URL dropped or pasted here is clipped into the vault and filed as a jot.
Jotting is not chattingIn Jot mode nothing replies. Your shorthand stays exactly as typed in a blush box; the enhancement paints in on the same row, anchored by typing time and meaning (E-tags, hollow when meaning only).
Ask without leaving the call⌘⏎ sends the line as a question instead. The answer goes to the block’s single assist slot and replaces the previous one; pinned answers move to the rail.
The block is the meetingCollapse it and it becomes one line in the conversation. Open it in the sheet and it becomes the full notes and transcript.
