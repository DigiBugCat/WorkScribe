Moments, not filesEach card is a place inside something: a speaker turn, a 30-second audio window, a frame every 3 seconds, a PDF page, a highlight in a web clip. Cards are buttons; the sheet opens at that exact place.
Answers say where they lookedThe meta line names the scope (meetings, folders, vault). Each source keeps its two switches — “during meetings” and “in Ask” — so a reply like “not the vault” narrows it.
Threads come from asking“Make this a thread” turns a line of inquiry into a filter in the rail (e.g. Photo retention) that keeps collecting new moments as they’re indexed.
