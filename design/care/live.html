The scene runs about three times faster than a real call: WorkScribe’s real debounce is 4 s and it rewrites at most once every 15 s.

0:02 – 0:07

It waits for you

Priya just said she’ll tell Halvorsen on Friday, so the “Ship date” line has news. You’re typing two lines below it, so the rewrite holds until you pause for two seconds. Nothing moves near your cursor while your hands are busy.

0:05 – 0:09

Never under the line you’re typing

“beta 40 accts” gets no AI line until you press Return. Then a hollow nib appears (it heard you), and the words arrive one at a time in pencil.

0:27

Only the changed words move

When “Photo queue” is rewritten, words that survive stay exactly where they are. Removed words fade out in place; new words arrive dark and dry to pencil grey over 2.4 s, so you can see what changed without a highlight.

0:21

A question doesn’t take the panel

Priya asks about the Halvorsen contract. The Transcript tab stays put; Live Q&A gets a count. The answer is waiting when you look, with its sources and where it was made.

0:34

Asked of you

When someone says your name and asks something, the card is marked “Asked of you” and sorted first. That’s the one you might need in the next ten seconds.

0:35

Listening for this

“android?” hasn’t come up yet. Its line says so once, in the faintest grey, and then stays quiet. If it never comes up, the line is simply left alone at Finalize.

Always

One clock

The menu bar, the pill and the status bar show the same tabular time, so the digits never jitter and the three never disagree by a second.

Why?

Hover a grey line for Why?. It opens the transcript at the moments the line came from. This works until the transcript fades; after Finalize the note stands on its own.
