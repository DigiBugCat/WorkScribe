Concept A · Workspace

WorkScribe as a standalone, full-window assistant on this Mac: one Obsidian-like workspace with a sidebar, where the home screen is a conversation with your meetings, notes, files and media. Meetings follow a live note workflow: shorthand on the left, enhanced notes appearing beside it live, a named transcript below, an AI sidecar on the right. The menu bar shrinks to a status item and notifications. Notes and meeting write-ups are Markdown in your Obsidian vault, and web clips from that vault are a source. Mockups to react to, not a spec.

Five screens

  1. 01Home is a conversation with your workChat with a morning briefing, cited answers with a hover preview, moments inside media. Rail: next call with the permission banner and Start, open items, recent, today's note.
  2. 02Meeting, liveShorthand beside live enhanced notes, the excerpt ruler for a selected line, the named transcript, the sidecar. Below it: the in-call companion and why it exists.
  3. 03Meeting reviewBrief, enhanced notes, missed topics, action items with owner and asker, answers, transcript with notes on the timeline, and the Markdown file in your vault.
  4. 04One search, returning momentsMeetings, vault notes and web clips, documents, audio, video, images. Preview plays the matched second; Ask instead turns it into chat.
  5. 05Menu bar and notificationsFour status-item states, a plain menu, five notifications with one action each, and their settings.

Open questions

  1. Vault as the store. Writing notes and write-ups as Markdown into a vault you pick is portable and gets Obsidian's links, graph and plugins for free. The costs: two apps editing one file (merge rules needed), anchors and excerpt ids must survive hand edits (block ids like ^e1), and transcripts and audio don't belong in a vault. Default to a vault, a plain folder, or WorkScribe's own library with export?
  2. Calendar. Home's “Next call” needs EventKit. Opt-in, with call detection as the fallback?
  3. Companion default. Opt-in here. Should it appear automatically when you're sharing your screen?
  4. Clips during calls. Web clips can be cited by live answers. Should the vault's clips default to “during meetings” off, like People ops?
