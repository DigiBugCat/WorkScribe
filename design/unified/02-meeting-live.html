The in-call companion

A 340 pt always-on-top window for when Zoom is in front or you're sharing your screen. It is the same meeting page, compressed: timer and levels, the last two notes with their enhanced lead, the jot line, and the answer slot reduced to one row. Companion in the title bar or ⌥⌘N pops it out; the main window can stay behind the call.

Why keep it. Moving off the menu bar removes the old popover's jot line. Without a small surface, jotting would mean switching away from the call. The floating window is also the one piece of today's WorkScribe people already use.

Why it's opt-in. It never opens by itself. Its enhancement is in place under each line, because two panes don't fit in 340 pt; the main window keeps side-by-side panes.
