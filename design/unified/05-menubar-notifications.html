Menu bar and notifications

The menu bar shrinks to a status item: is it recording, for how long, and does something need you. Clicking it opens a plain menu, not a popover. Everything that used to live in the popover is either in the window or arrives as a macOS notification with one action.

A menu, not a popover

Six rows, all of them verbs. No timeline, no search, no jot line. The jot line moved to the companion window, search and Ask moved to the main window, and the post-meeting digest became the “Recording saved” notification below.

Why not drop the status item entirely? Because “is it recording right now?” has to be answerable without switching apps, and that was the most important finding of the UX review.

The dot is drawn by the recording state above; in this menu figure it's left neutral so the figure has no second vermilion.

Five notifications, each with one job

Standard macOS banners (UserNotifications), with action buttons. Content is kept short so it's safe on a shared screen; nothing from your notes or files appears in a banner while you're sharing.
