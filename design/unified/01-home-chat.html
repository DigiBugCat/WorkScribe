Chat is the front doorThe first thing you see is a conversation, not a dashboard. A daily briefing comes in as the chat's first message, so it's quotable, cited, and you can reply to it.
Today sits around itThe right rail holds what's time-bound: the next call and whether it will record properly, open items with who asked, recent meetings with failures explained. It collapses below the chat on a narrow window.
One vermilionThe seal is on the one thing that needs you before 10:00: system audio is off. The due-tomorrow item gets a blush field, not a second seal.
